Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Autoroid in Pakistan.

Does Autoroid require rooting my Google Pixel? +

No! Autoroid is designed to run seamlessly with Shizuku over Wireless ADB. You do not need to unlock your bootloader, flash Magisk, or root your phone. Your warranty remains 100% intact, and Google Play Protect / SafetyNet will not be triggered.

Will this work on CPID, Non-PTA, or PTA-Approved Pixels? +

Yes, 100%. Autoroid operates at the Android carrier configuration and baseband signaling layer. It works identically on PTA-approved devices, official eSIMs, physical SIMs, CPID-patched devices, and non-PTA devices.

Will Pakistani banking apps (SadaPay, NayaPay, Easypaisa) block my phone? +

No. Unlike older automation tools that cause banking apps to refuse opening, Autoroid includes Bank Mode (Accessibility Cloak). It wipes active accessibility service traces while you bank, ensuring SadaPay, NayaPay, HBL, Meezan, and Easypaisa open without any warnings.

How does Autoroid bypass Google's CVE-2025-48617 restriction? +

On Android 14, 15, and 16, Google's CarrierConfigLoader blocks shell processes from calling overrideConfig(). Autoroid solves this via Two-Tier Privileged Instrumentation: it launches a privileged background instrumentation session (ImsModifier / BrokerInstrumentation) that delegates shell permission identity, safely setting the carrier bundle and injecting hardware NVRAM provisioning.

Do I need to re-apply the carrier patch every time I reboot my phone? +

No! Autoroid includes a built-in BootReceiver that automatically wakes upon device startup and reapplies your carrier configuration overrides in the background seamlessly.

Is Autoroid free and open-source? +

Yes, 100% free and open-source under the Apache 2.0 License. There are no subscriptions, no locked features, and no ads. You can inspect the entire Kotlin and C++ source code on our GitHub repository.
